# EU disclosure icons

Official European Commission SVGs downloaded 10 September 2026 from:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content

SVG archive: https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/129546

Files are copied unchanged from that archive, with local filenames:
- eu-basic-{black,white}.svg: LABEL_AI_{black,white}.svg
- eu-generated-{black,white}.svg: LABEL_AI GENERATED_{black,white}.svg
- eu-modified-{black,white}.svg: LABEL_AI MODIFIED_{black,white}.svg

Mapping: involved → basic; generated → fully AI-generated; modified → partially AI-modified.
The 50% variants apply opacity to the matching official color asset.
The Commission permits free use without attribution. Icons alone do not establish legal compliance.
